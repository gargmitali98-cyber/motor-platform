Garage Portal — the journey

Garage Portal PRD V9 · 21 Oct 2026 · what the panel garage sees and does

A panel garage takes an Al Koot repair job from request to payment. Every field, photo, document and decision it submits appears on the claim in Claims Core, and every Al Koot request comes back as a status, a task and a notification.

Amber diamonds = a decision or approval. Each step opens as its own tab.
One login per garage · Garage Manager, full accessOne ID · Claim No. AK-CLM-YYYY-NNNNNNNN — no FNOL numberAgencies · no login unless "Uses Garage Portal" = Yes
Only the garagechanges the estimate — surveyor, CM or HoC ask; the garage revises with justification and cannot reject
No querieson the transfer letter, LPO, invoice or subrogation letter — handled with Al Koot outside the system
Other outcomesTotal loss (vehicle to the yard) or cash settlement (estimate only) instead of repair
Everything loggedJob History in the portal and the audit trail in Claims Core, both directions
STEP 1

Request & accept

New Request → Vehicle Receipt Pending · Rejected — Closed · Request Closed

Al Koot allocates the garage and the surveyor together and sends the transfer letter. The garage decides on capability only. A walk-in FNOL accepted by Al Koot skips this step.

The request opens the accept / reject view — no other action until the garage decides.

STEP 3

Estimate & survey

Estimate Pending → Estimate Under Review ⇄ Re-estimation Required → Estimate Approved

The garage prices the repair as line items (or a lump sum / hardcopy). Only the garage changes the estimate: the surveyor disputes lines with a ceiling and reason, the garage revises with justification. The approved report then goes to the Surveyor Manager and the Head of Claims.

Every round is a new version in Job History.

STEP 4

LPO & outcome

Estimate Approved → LPO Acceptance Pending → Repair In Progress · or Total Loss / Estimate Only

After the survey report is approved the system drafts the LPO; the Claims Handler or surveyor edits it and the Head of Claims approves. The garage accepts the LPO — there is no query option. The surveyor may instead recommend total loss or cash settlement.

A returned LPO stays inside Al Koot — the garage keeps waiting.

VehicleMake, model, body, usage, year, registration, chassis (masked), engine, delivery period

STEP 5

Repair & supplementary

Repair In Progress → Supplementary Under Review ⇄ Re-estimation → Revised LPO → Ready for Delivery

The garage updates milestones as it repairs. Hidden damage found after dismantling goes in a supplementary; once the surveyor approves and the Head of Claims signs the revised LPO, the new LPO appears in the portal.

Milestones are progress data in Claims Core, not statuses.

STEP 6

Delivery & excess

Ready for Delivery → Excess Payment Pending → Delivered · Subrogation Re-upload

The garage completes the checklist, sends the excess payment link and hands over once the excess is paid (or collected in cash if the link fails) and the customer has signed the subrogation letter.

The garage is paid the full approved amount — the excess goes to Al Koot.

STEP 7

Invoice & payment

Invoice Due → Under Validation ⇄ Invoice Revision → Approved for Payment → Paid — Closed

The garage invoices up to LPO + approved supplementary. The surveyor verifies line by line and can ask for a revision (never a rejection); the Claims Handler prepares the settlement and the Head of Claims approves. Finance pays and the Claims Handler closes.

Payment SLA: 30 days from invoice submission (view only).

OTHER FLOW

Walk-in FNOL

Submitted — Awaiting Al Koot → Accepted (Claim No.) · Rejected · Information Requested

The garage reports a loss for a walk-in customer. A Claims Handler always reviews it (no STP); once accepted it becomes a claim with this garage and starts at vehicle receipt.

Until accepted the FNOL stays in the FNOL list, never in the Claims queue.

OTHER FLOW

Agency mode

Uses Garage Portal = No → Claims Handler works the portal for the agency

An agency without portal access does not log in: the Claims Handler opens the claim in the Garage Portal in agency mode and records what the agency sends by email. Statuses and approvals are the same; only the checks are relaxed.

An agency with portal access uses the portal exactly like a panel garage.
