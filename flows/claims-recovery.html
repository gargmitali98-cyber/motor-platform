Claims recovery — four tracks

CC-07 Version 3 · after the claim is settled · no integration with insurers, reinsurers or cedants

Two tracks bring money in (outbound, FAC outward) and two pay money out (inbound, FAC inward). Everything happens by email, but every mail, status and payment is recorded in Claims Core.

Blue lanes recover money for Al Koot; orange lanes pay money out.
3 claims logins · CH does the work, HoC approves, CM viewsFinance · own login — pays inbound and FAC inwardEmail only · mails sent from the system and logged
After settlementRecovery notice goes to the other party only after the claim is settled (BRD-FNOL-20)
Inbound is a claimNot a recovery — created by the CH from the insurer's documents, no FNOL
Netting OffBoth parties with Al Koot → internal settlement between sub-claims, no mail, no cash
TRACK 1

Outbound recovery

Recovery Raised → Pending with Insurer → Partially / Fully Recovered → Closed

Al Koot's insured was not at fault. Once the claim is paid, Al Koot recovers the cost (paid × third-party liability %) from the other insurer by email, with the invoice, credit note, approved estimate, police report and subrogation letter.

Full payment recorded with a document closes the case on its own.

TRACK 2

Inbound — insurer-notified claim

Created by CH → optional survey → HoC approves → Finance pays → Closed

Al Koot's insured was at fault and the other insurer mails a demand with its documents. Al Koot handles it as a direct claim: the FIR finds an existing claim to map to, or a new claim is created without FNOL. No garage, repair or LPO.

Survey uses the same maker-checker as the claims journey: surveyor → Surveyor Manager → Head of Claims.

TRACK 3

Flag at registration → claim unchanged → case per reinsurer → recovered / shortfall

Al Koot wrote the policy and ceded a share facultatively. The claim journey does not change; on settlement a case per reinsurer recovers settled net × share (BRD Workflow 10).

Shares must reconcile to the total ceded % before the pack goes out.

TRACK 4

Facultative inward

Register → verify period, documents, share → HoC → Finance pays cedant

Another insurer (the cedant) wrote the policy and Al Koot accepted a share. Al Koot follows the cedant's settlement — checks the arrangement, the documents and the share, then pays (BRD Workflow 11).

In Qatar all documents arrive together, so only verification and settlement — no technical reserve.
