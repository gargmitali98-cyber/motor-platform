Claims recovery — four tracks

CC-07 Version 3 · after the claim is settled · no integration with other insurers

Two tracks bring money in (outbound, FAC outward) and two pay money out (inbound, FAC inward). Everything happens by email, but every mail, status and payment is recorded in Claims Core.

Blue lanes recover money for Al Koot; orange lanes pay money out.
3 claims logins · CH does the work, HoC approves, CM viewsDebit / credit note · per case — the source of truth; no money per caseBulk UTR · net per insurer at quarter / year endEmail only · mails sent from the system and logged
No transfer per caseInsurers keep every claim on debit / credit notes and settle the net in bulk at quarter / year end. The CH records the note on each case and the UTR once per insurer per period.
High-value FACFAC outward claim ≥ QAR 100,000: every co-insurer is notified before Al Koot settles

TRACK 1

Outbound recovery

Recovery Raised → Pending with Insurer → Partially / Fully Recovered (DN / CN) → bulk settled → Closed

Al Koot's insured was not at fault. Once the claim is paid, Al Koot recovers the cost (paid × third-party liability %) from the other insurer by email, with the invoice, credit note, approved estimate, police report and subrogation letter. When the insurer agrees, the Claims Handler records a debit or credit note — no money moves per case; the net with each insurer is settled in bulk at quarter / year end.

Notes for the full amount mark the case Fully Recovered; it closes when the quarter / year bulk settlement (UTR) is recorded.

TRACK 3

Facultative outward

Flag at registration → high-value notice before settlement → case per follower → DN / CN → bulk settled

Al Koot is the leader: it insures the vehicle together with other insurers (followers) to reduce its risk. When a claim is paid, Al Koot pays the full amount and recovers each follower's share — one case per follower. A claim of QAR 100,000 or more is notified to every co-insurer before Al Koot settles it. Each share is marked by a debit / credit note and settled in bulk with the follower at quarter / year end. The claim journey does not change (BRD Workflow 10 calls the followers reinsurers).

TRACK 4

Facultative inward

Register → verify period, documents, share → HoC → credit / debit note → bulk settled

The opposite of FAC outward: another insurer is the leader and Al Koot is a follower with a share. The leader settles the claim and calls Al Koot's share; Al Koot checks the arrangement, the documents and the share, then records a credit / debit note; the net with the leader is settled in bulk at quarter / year end (BRD Workflow 11 calls the leader the cedant).

SETTLEMENT

Bulk settlement — quarter / year end

Debit / credit note per case → net per insurer → one UTR

Insurers do not transfer money claim by claim. Each case — outbound, FAC outward, inbound and FAC inward — carries a debit or credit note recorded by the Claims Handler; that note is the source of truth. At quarter or year end the receivables and payables with each insurer are netted and settled in one transfer, and its UTR is entered once.

The UTR must be unique and equal the net of the selected notes; every note in the batch is stamped with it.
