FNOL

Intimated → FNOL Received → FNOL Accepted

Capture the loss, check the policy, collect documents and turn an accepted FNOL into a claim. FNOLs from the garage, broker and customer portals land in the same queue for the Claims Handler.

STEP 2

Claim creation & reserve

Registered → Reserve Set / Reserve Approval Pending

Register the claim from the accepted FNOL: set severity per party, build the components and post a reserve on each. A claim cannot exist without a reserve; the new high-value damage band (50,000) needs approval.

Reserve bands (QAR, fixed — per CC-04)

SeverityComponentBand · amount
Minor / Moderate / Major ODOwn Damage1 · 1,000 / 2 · 5,000 / 3 · 10,000
Catastrophe (tag)Own Damage4 · 50,000
High-value damage (no catastrophe)Own DamageNew · 50,000 CM / HoC approval
Total loss candidateOwn Damage5 · SI or market value, lower
Minor / Major property damageTP Property (insured at fault)1 · 1,000 / 3 · 10,000
Minor / serious injury · permanent disabilityTP Bodily Injury6 · 20,000
DeathTP Bodily Injury7 · 200,000 per person (automatic)

Example: Moderate OD 5,000 + TP Major PD 10,000 + Minor injury 20,000 = 35,000

No claim without a reserveOne reserve per component; parent shows the totalCustomer SMS on creation

Manual handlingFault unknown or not 100% → tag, processed outside the portal
Same-insurer partySub-claim AK-CLM-…-NN, own journey, linked to parent

STEP 3

Garage & surveyor allocation

Assessment → Garage & Surveyor Allocated → Vehicle Receipt Pending

Send the claim to a workshop and a surveyor. The agency rule decides the route: panel garage, agency (run by the Claims Handler over email) or an agency override approved by the Head of Claims.

Route decided by the agency rule

3.1 Panel garage

Garage → transfer letter → surveyor → submit. Nothing reaches the garage until the surveyor is selected.

3.2 Agency — CH in agency mode

Email only · mandatory: final estimate, survey report, LPO · no supplementary
Garage rejectsCapability reason → CH reallocates, new letter
3.3 OverrideNot eligible but customer wants agency → justification → HoC approves; claim waits
ReallocationSurveyor any stage · garage before LPO · after LPO handled manually (phase 1)
Letter expiry30 days from sending → CH re-issues

STEP 5

Estimate & re-estimation

Estimate Submitted ⇄ Re-estimation Requested → Approved

The AI damage assessment runs when the garage submits the inspection and is the baseline the garage builds its estimate on. The garage prices the repair and the surveyor works it line by line until an amount is approved. Only the garage changes the estimate; the back office watches the variance against the reserve.

AI baseline runs on inspection submit · lines AI or edited by garageOnly the garage changes the estimateVariance vs reserve flagged
Re-estimation loopGarage accepts the revision or sends it back with justification — it cannot reject the request
ReturnedWrong claim no. / plate → back to garage · document issue → back to CH, customer notified
Total-loss riskEstimate > 75% IDV → band 5

Repair In Progress → Revised LPO → Ready for Delivery

The garage repairs and posts milestones. Hidden damage comes in as a supplementary: the surveyor reviews it, the LPO is regenerated and the Head of Claims approves the revised LPO.

Ready for Delivery → Excess Payment Pending → Delivery Documents Under Review

The vehicle goes back to the customer once the excess is settled and the subrogation letter is signed. Back office reviews the delivery documents and can send the subrogation letter back.

Link failsGarage collects cash, marks it → deducted from its payment
Customer refusesVehicle not released; CH informed

STEP 10

Invoice

Invoice Submitted → Surveyor Verified → HoC Approved

The garage invoices against the LPO. The surveyor verifies it against the approved estimate, the Claims Handler prepares the settlement and the Head of Claims approves it for payment.

Revision loopSurveyor asks revision → garage resubmits · never rejected
TimingNot settled before the workshop's delivery date · KYC / AML for third parties

STEP 11

Payment & closure

Approved for Payment → Payment Processed → Closed – Settled

Finance pays from its own login. The Claims Handler records the payment reference and closes the claim — nothing closes automatically.

No auto-close on payment
Payment failedFinance retries; claim stays in Payment
Partial settlementOpen component keeps its reserve; parent closes last
After closureReopen (CH → CM / HoC) · auto-close 3 years after intimation · fraud reinstatement
