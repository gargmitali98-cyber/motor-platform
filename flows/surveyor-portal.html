Surveyor Portal — the journey

Surveyor Portal PRD V2 · 21 Oct 2026 · every claim needs a survey

The surveyor accepts an allocated claim, chooses the survey type, reviews the garage's evidence and estimate, works it with the garage until it can be approved, and submits the survey report. The Surveyor Manager approves every report before the Head of Claims. The surveyor also edits the LPO draft and verifies the final invoice.

Amber diamonds = a decision or approval. Each step opens as its own tab.

ReserveFollows the LPO approved by the Head of Claims, not the survey amount
Hidden from surveyorCustomer QID and contact · internal notes · fraud flags
Fraud holdAccept, schedule and approve are blocked until the hold is lifted
STEP 1

Assignment & survey type

New assignment → Assigned — awaiting estimate

The Claims Handler allocates the garage and surveyor together and recommends a survey type. The surveyor accepts — or asks the Claims Handler for a revision with a reason; there is no decline — then picks the type and books the visit or call.

Physical surveys need the vehicle at the garage first.

STEP 2

Evidence & estimate review

Estimate to review ⇄ Awaiting resubmission · Info / re-capture requested

The garage's inspection (20 named photos, video, verification) is visible as soon as it is submitted. The AI damage assessment runs then and is the baseline the garage builds its estimate on; the estimate review opens only when the garage submits the estimate, which starts the survey clock.

The AI assessment arrives with the estimate — each line shows AI or Edited by garage. It is for reference only and never approves.

STEP 3

Survey report & approval

Report submitted → Pending Surveyor Manager → Pending HoC · Returned · Revision requested

The surveyor submits the survey report with a recommendation. The Surveyor Manager approves or returns it; the Head of Claims then approves or — with the Claims Manager — asks for changes. The Claims Handler only views it.

After approval: repair → LPO draft · total loss → Committee · cash settlement → HoC approves the amount.

STEP 4

LPO draft

LPO draft → Draft LPO – Pending HoC → LPO Issued · LPO Returned

Once the Head of Claims approves the report, the system drafts the LPO from the approved lines. The surveyor or the Claims Handler edits it and saves it for approval; only the Head of Claims approves (US-SUR-01 A).

The same flow runs for a revised LPO after an approved supplementary.
ItemRule
Who editsSurveyor or Claims Handler · every save is a version with editor and time

LimitsLines ≤ approved estimate; reductions allowed; excess and policy fields read-only
ReturnedComments to both; either edits and resubmits
IssuedGarage sees it; reserve = LPO amount; surveyor read-only

STEP 5

Supplementary

Supplementary review → Revised LPO – Pending HoC · Challenged · Rejected

Hidden damage found during repair comes back as a supplementary. The surveyor approves, challenges or rejects it; an approved supplementary regenerates the LPO, which the Head of Claims approves.

The garage sees the revised LPO only after Head of Claims approval.
Challenge loopRe-estimation with a reason → garage revises the supplementary
RejectedGarage repairs on the original scope
Second supplementaryTriggers a total-loss check by Al Koot

STEP 6

Invoice verification

Invoice – Surveyor Verification → Invoice – Pending HoC · Invoice Revision Requested

The surveyor checks the garage's final invoice line by line against LPO + approved supplementary. Accept, or ask for a revision with a reason — there is no reject (US-SUR-01 B).

The screen shows LPO + supplementary vs invoiced amount per line with the variance, plus the invoice and delivery documents.

STEP 7

Total loss & cash settlement

Total Loss – Pending HoC / Committee → Vehicle at the Yard · Cash Settlement – Pending HoC → Approved

When repair is not the right route the surveyor submits a total-loss report or recommends cash settlement. Both still go through the Surveyor Manager and the Head of Claims.

On total loss the chassis is blacklisted and the policy is cancelled without refund.

OTHER FLOW

Agency claims

Uses Garage Portal = No · survey still mandatory

An agency without portal access works by email; the Claims Handler uploads what it sends. The survey clock starts when the Claims Handler uploads the final estimate, and the standard evidence checks are not enforced (US-AGY-01).

Approvals are identical to a panel garage claim.
ItemRule
Survey typePhysical (at the agency), virtual or self-assessment
Evidence16-photo and angle checklist not enforced
Estimate document onlySurveyor enters the approved amount (lines or lump sum)
Re-estimation · re-captureHidden — "Agency claim — contact by email"
ReportEvidence basis mandatory (agency email · physical survey · both) · evidence gaps
MetricsMissing standard evidence not counted against the surveyor
